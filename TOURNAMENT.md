# Tournament — 24 candidate angles for the GTA VI trophy guide

Scored pairwise against the kit judge rubric, in order:
(1) tracker and its data, (2) roadmap and missables built from the official
descriptions, (3) cited guidance in our own words, (4) fast, clear, polished,
small first load. Each pairing below was decided on the first criterion where
the two differed, unless a later criterion differed by much more.

## Candidates

1. Plain Table Ledger — starter-style table, longer guides pasted in.
2. Card Wall — one big card per trophy, guide inside the card.
3. Leonida Ledger — split pane: checklist left, full guide panel right.
4. Map-First Atlas — fake map hero, trophies pinned under it.
5. Chapter Novel — guide as one long scrolling essay, tracker at the end.
6. Speedrun Strip — single column ordered by fastest earn time.
7. Grade Pyramid — trophies grouped in a platinum/gold/silver pyramid.
8. Newspaper Front Page — Leonida broadsheet styling, dense columns.
9. Checklist Minimal — bare ticks and names, guides behind a modal.
10. Roadmap River — one vertical timeline carrying every trophy.
11. Missables Alarm — red-alert theme built around missable warnings.
12. Collector Binder — binder-page look aimed at collectible counts.
13. Duo Diary — Jason-and-Lucia alternating diary entries per trophy.
14. Search Palace — giant search box, everything else secondary.
15. Tab Deck — one tab per kind, no combined view.
16. Progress Ring Hero — huge percentage ring, list below the fold.
17. Night Neon Ledger — Ledger layout in a neon night-palette identity.
18. Print Pamphlet — black-and-white printable booklet styling.
19. Phone Companion — narrow mobile column stretched to desktop.
20. Trophy Cabinet — shelf-and-cabinet visual metaphor, heavy graphics.
21. Stage Gates — roadmap stages as locked gates that open in order.
22. Dual Ledger Pro — Ledger plus roadmap and missables as switchable views.
23. Spoiler Vault — spoiler masking as the headline feature, rest plain.
24. Source Margin — wide margin column carrying every citation visibly.

## Round of 24 (winner first)

- Leonida Ledger beat Plain Table Ledger (guide visible beside the list, crit. 1/3)
- Card Wall beat Map-First Atlas (no borrowed map imagery, crit. 4)
- Chapter Novel beat Speedrun Strip (chapter order matches descriptions, crit. 2)
- Grade Pyramid beat Newspaper Front Page (grades scannable at a glance, crit. 1)
- Roadmap River beat Checklist Minimal (roadmap is a scored view, crit. 2)
- Missables Alarm beat Collector Binder (missable flags headline, crit. 2)
- Search Palace beat Duo Diary (finding a trophy takes a glance, crit. 4)
- Tab Deck beat Progress Ring Hero (filters visible, not buried, crit. 1)
- Night Neon Ledger beat Print Pamphlet (coherent own identity, crit. 4)
- Trophy Cabinet beat Phone Companion (desktop judge view, crit. 4)
- Stage Gates beat Spoiler Vault (spoiler setting survives inside gates, crit. 1)
- Dual Ledger Pro beat Source Margin (citations stay in the guide panel, crit. 3)

## Quarter-finals

- Leonida Ledger beat Card Wall — one screen shows list, progress and guide.
- Roadmap River beat Grade Pyramid — order to platinum beats grade grouping.
- Missables Alarm beat Search Palace — flags built from descriptions win crit. 2.
- Dual Ledger Pro beat Night Neon Ledger — same identity, more views covered.

## Semi-finals

- Dual Ledger Pro beat Leonida Ledger — the Ledger is its checklist view, and
  it adds the roadmap and missables views the rubric scores separately.
- Dual Ledger Pro beat Roadmap River and Missables Alarm in the final
  three-way: it keeps the river's stage order inside its Roadmap view and the
  alarm's flags inside its Missables view, without giving up the tracker.

## Winner

**Dual Ledger Pro, shipped as "Leonida Ledger — Platinum Route".**
Split-pane ledger: progress header with per-grade counts; checklist with
kind / grade / earned filters and search; a guide panel that always shows the
selected trophy's steps, missable warning and named source; switchable
Roadmap and Missables views; spoiler masking for story trophies; progress in
localStorage with export and import. Identity: deep teal-and-sand "ledger"
palette, ruled-paper rows, grade chips — no game artwork, system fonts only.
